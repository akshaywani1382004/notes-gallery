Notes Gallery

Notes Gallery is a canvas for your notes. Add blocks, move them around, and open a block to find another canvas inside it.

Workspaces

  • The app opens on your list of workspaces. Each one is a separate canvas.
  • Click a workspace to open it. Click the logo (top left) to go back.
  • Use New workspace to make one, or Import to open a saved file.
  • Export saves a workspace to one file (with your notes and images) so you can back it up or move it to another device.

Adding & editing

  • Add — click Add (top right) and pick Block, List, Text, Shape or Image. Or double-click an empty spot.
  • Open a block — double-click it, or use the arrow button. This shows the canvas inside it.
  • Edit — click a block once to select it, then click the pencil to open the editor. Changes save on their own.
  • Connect — click the link button, then click two blocks. Click a line to remove it.
  • Attach files — upload PDFs or images in the editor, then view or download them any time.
  • Import — drag a file onto the canvas: an image becomes a picture, a .txt/.md becomes text, a .csv becomes a list.

Moving around

  • Move a block — drag it. Move the view — drag an empty area.
  • Zoom — mouse wheel, or pinch on a touch screen. Fit — the frame button shows everything.
  • Select many — Shift-click blocks, or right-drag a box around them. Dragging one moves the whole group.
  • Layer order — right-click a block to bring it forward or send it back.
