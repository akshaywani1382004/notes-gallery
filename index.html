Notes Gallery

Notes Gallery is a canvas for your notes. Add blocks, move them around, and open a block to find another canvas inside it.

Workspaces

  • The app opens on your list of workspaces. Each one is a separate canvas.
  • Click a workspace to open it. Click the logo (top left) to go back.
  • Use New workspace to make one, or Import to open a saved file.
  • Export saves a workspace to one file (with your notes and images) so you can back it up or move it to another device.

Adding & editing

  • Add — click Add and pick Block, List, Text, Shape, Checkbox or Import.
  • Checkbox — a tap ticks or unticks it; drag its corner grip to size it; double-tap for its colour.
  • Open a block — double-click it, or use the arrow button. This shows the canvas inside it.
  • Edit — click a block once to select it, then click the pencil to open the editor. Changes save on their own.
  • Connect — click the link button, then click two blocks. Click a line to remove it.
  • Attach files — upload PDFs or images in the editor, then view or download them any time.
  • Import — drag a file onto the canvas: an image becomes a picture, a .txt/.md becomes text, a .csv becomes a list.

Moving around

  • Move a block — drag it. Move the view — drag an empty area.
  • Zoom — mouse wheel, or pinch on a touch screen. Fit — in the ⋯ menu, shows everything.
  • Select many — Shift-click blocks, or right-drag a box around them. Dragging one moves the whole group.
  • Layer order — right-click a block to bring it forward or send it back.
  • Lock a direction — the ⋯ menu can lock panning to horizontal or vertical, so a long page does not drift sideways.

Writing by hand

  • Draw — the pen button opens draw mode. The pen always writes: over empty paper, over blocks, over earlier strokes. Two fingers pan and zoom.
  • Stylus only — the pen, eraser and Select tool answer to the stylus, and fingers move the page. On a device with no stylus, tap the hand in the draw panel to let fingers draw too; it stays how you set it.
  • Erase — the eraser works with or without the draw panel. It has three modes, picked from the small dots on its button (or by holding the toolbar button): Eraser rubs out just the part you sweep over, Stroke eraser removes a whole stroke at a touch, and Erase with selection removes everything you circle.
  • Pen styles — the dots on the pen button switch between pencil, pen, brush and highlighter; the button shows the one in use.
  • The panel — drag it from anywhere on its body. It snaps to the middle of any edge or into a corner; along the top or bottom it lies flat, down a side it stands up, and in a corner it keeps whichever way it was.
  • Select — circle anything with the Select tool to pick it up; the dots on its button switch between New, Add and Remove, and the icon shows which. A tap on handwriting picks up the whole word without the tool; once picked up it can be dragged. A dashed frame appears round it; drag the corner grip to make the writing bigger or smaller. The bar above it groups, opens properties, copies, cuts, deletes and changes the layer order; alignment lives in Ctrl+K.
  • Menu on hold — press and hold with a finger or the stylus (outside draw mode) to open the same menu a right-click gives.
