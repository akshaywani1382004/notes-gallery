Notes Gallery

A workspace of blocks on an infinite canvas. Every block holds notes and files, can connect to others, and can open into its own inner canvas — nest as deep as you like.

Workspaces

  • The app opens on your workspaces. Each is a separate canvas.
  • New / Import from that screen; each card has properties, export, delete.
  • Click the logo to return there; hover the logo inside a workspace to switch quickly.
  • Export workspace makes one portable .json (with your files) — import it anywhere to reopen it.

Blocks & lists

  • Add — the + Add button (Block, List, Text or Shape), or double-click empty canvas.
  • Block = a card on a free canvas. List = rows that are each blocks; its card previews what's inside.
  • Select — single-click. Open inside — double-click, or the → button.
  • Edit — the ✎ button (on hover/selection) opens the side editor; it autosaves.
  • Connect — the link tool, then tap two blocks; click a line to remove it.
  • Attach — upload PDFs, images, diagrams in the editor; view or download them anytime.
  • Images — Add → Image, drag & drop files onto the canvas, or paste (Ctrl+V) a copied picture / screenshot.
  • Layering — right-click a block → bring forward / send backward / to front / to back (Ctrl+] / [, add Shift for all-the-way).

Selecting & moving

  • Multi-select — Shift+click, or right-drag a box across blocks.
  • Drag any selected block to move the whole group. Copy/paste deep-copies everything inside.
  • Pan — drag empty space. Zoom — wheel or pinch. Fit frames everything.
